Private by design

Members only, by invitation

iShul is not a public social network. Your app is a closed space for your community — no one outside the shul can see your members, your board, or who's coming to dinner.

Invite code
SHUL26
Issued by your shul · No code, no way in

Access by invite code

Downloading the app isn't enough. A member gets in only by entering a code your shul gave them — so the app stays your community, and only your community.

Members see only members

Posts, needs, simchas, and who's attending are visible inside your shul alone — never public, never searchable on the web, never shared with another community.

Your list, your control

Issue codes to new families and remove access for anyone who leaves — all from your dashboard, so the membership is always yours to decide.

Every member also verifies their own phone number when they join, and each shul's community is kept completely separate from every other.
